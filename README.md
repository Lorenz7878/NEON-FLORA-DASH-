🌸 Neon Flora Dash 🚀
Neon Flora Dash is a fast-paced, feature-rich sci-fi endless runner built entirely with HTML5 Canvas and Vanilla JavaScript. Pilot your customizable spacecraft through treacherous cosmic zones, dodge moving obstacles, survive meteor showers, and defeat deadly bosses!

✨ Features
Dynamic Environments: Fly through distinct zones like the Nebula, Asteroid Belt, Ionosphere, and Deep Void. Each zone introduces unique hazards, from dynamic wind currents to visual fog.

Unique Spaceships: Unlock and choose between 4 different ships, each with its own special ability:

🚀 Rocket: The reliable standard ship.

🛸 UFO: Anti-gravity hover stabilizer.

🛩️ Shuttle: Double-tap for a high-speed forward Dash.

🐉 Dragon: Triggers cinematic Slow-Mo during near-misses.

Procedural Audio Engine: A custom-built Web Audio API synthesizer generates real-time music beats, dynamic engine hums, and sound effects with sidechain ducking.

"Ghost" Replay System: The game records your best runs at 20fps, allowing you to race against the ghost of your past high scores.

Power-ups & Combat: Collect coins to buy permanent upgrades or pick up in-game power-ups like Shields, Magnets, Time-Slow, and Lasers to destroy obstacles and meteors.

Endless Retention: Features Daily Runs with seeded RNG, Hardcore Challenges, lifetime stat tracking, and a local leaderboard system.

🎮 How to Play
Controls
Thrust (Fly Up): Hold Spacebar, Click, or Tap and hold the screen.

Fall (Gravity): Release the button/screen.

Pause: Press Escape (or unfocus the window).

Special Abilities: Double-tap Spacebar or the screen (if playing as the Shuttle).

The Goal
Survive as long as possible by flying through the gaps in the neon pillars. Collect coins to build your combo multiplier, unlock new ships, and buy permanent upgrades in the Shop. Watch out for the Boss phase!

🛠️ Tech Stack
Frontend: HTML5 Canvas, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+).

Audio: Native Web Audio API (No external audio libraries used).

Storage: localStorage for saving high scores, unlocked characters, shop items, and daily challenge progress.

Assets: Custom SVG/Canvas fallback drawing for missing assets.
